PROJECT OVERVIEW:
Coding Battle Arena is a futuristic, AAA Esports-style competitive coding platform
where programmers duel in real-time 1v1 multiplayer matches, practice against AI bots,
and solve algorithmic challenges featuring legendary Anime Heroes, live ELO ranks,
and sandboxed execution.
